# Creative direction

A direction is a decision with a reason. A mood board is not a direction.

## Shape of a direction

Write it in this form:

```
Name           Two or three words that are memorable and specific
Premise        One sentence: the idea the whole piece expresses
Because        Why this, for this audience, in this category
Not            The obvious alternative, and why it loses
Expressed as   Type, colour, imagery, motion, layout - one line each
Signature      The one thing someone would describe to a friend
```

If `Name` is "Modern Minimal" you have not arrived yet. If `Because` restates the
premise, you have not arrived yet.

## Three, then one

Generate three different directions, not three shades of one. Different
means they disagree about something - what the piece is for, what it borrows from, what
it refuses.

Then pick one and say why, in writing. Present the other two in the case study as
rejected, with the reason. That is the part that reads as studio work.

`prototype` will put versions side by side if you want to see them before choosing.
`impeccable` is the right skill to argue with once you have a candidate - ask it to
critique, not to generate.

## Making it specific

Vague direction produces average output. Each of these should have a value, not an
adjective:

- **Type** - named faces, the scale ratio, and the one typographic move that carries
  the personality. "Tight negative tracking on very large display type" is a move.
  "Good typography" is not.
- **Colour** - the count, the ratio in use, and the accent's single job. Three colours
  used in a 70/25/5 ratio reads designed. Eight colours reads undecided.
- **Imagery** - subject, lighting direction, grade, crop. Enough that two people
  generating assets separately would produce a matching set.
- **Layout** - the grid, and what breaks it. A system with no deliberate break is
  inert; one with breaks everywhere is noise.
- **Motion** - the personality in one word, and the signature move.
- **Texture and depth** - grain, paper, glass, flat. Say none if none.

## The signature

One thing per project that is memorable and specific to it. A material that reacts to
the cursor. A scroll that reveals a cross-section. A wordmark that resolves on load.
Everything else stays quiet so it lands.

Three signature moments is zero signature moments.

## Checking it

Before building: read the direction next to the clichés list from research. If any line
of the direction appears on the clichés list, it is not a direction, it is the default.

After building: `taste-skill` and `impeccable` both exist to tell you the output went
generic. Use them before the user does.
